Garage Evolution 1.5.1

New in 1.5.1:
- Loads about five times faster: you start scrubbing while the rest of the garages' cars arrive.

New in 1.5.0:
- Non-stop garage: the next car rolls in right after RESTORED; your pay counts up over the garage and new models fly into your album.
- Garage fund: part of every job is saved for your next garage, so your goal only grows.
- Robots earn from the very first hire — also while you're away.
- Rush jobs: beat the clock and the job pays double!
- One-tap upgrades in a gold button next to the car; new tools are shown off.
- Tyre bay jobs are shorter, and each car brings one or two jobs, not all of them.
- Meet the rare car of the day in your first session, and see tomorrow's waiting.
- Coming back: one tap collects your daily reward and your robots' earnings.
- Phones on their side: bigger cars, buttons down the side.

In 1.4:
- 120 cars, every one a detailed painting, and 3 new garages: 23 in all.
- Your garages are kept when you come back; a new garage opens once enough cars are restored in your newest one.
- The mechanic robot finishes the wheels by itself; spaceplanes fly in through the door.
