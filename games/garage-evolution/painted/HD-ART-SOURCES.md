# High-definition vehicle art integration (2026-11-04)

30 vehicles were matched against their original transparent high-resolution side-view artworks generated for Idle Garage Evolution (October 17-18, 2026). Their compressed low-resolution WebP textures have been replaced without changing model IDs, original geometry metadata, collision/hit masks, axle positions, paint logic or game mechanics.

The replacement consists of 70 high-quality WebP vehicle and wheel sprites. Dimensions are generally three times the prior body resolution; images were transformed from genuine original PNGs rather than artificially enlarged old textures.

Models: brezza, atlas, halo, kaminari, static, talon, homolog, brawler, wisp, shootingstar, bigcat, heritage, palace, saber, tempest, ember, gecko, prism, carbonsix, apparition, dart, propeller, autobahn, ridgeback, cinq, orca, verdant, squadra, pebble, inferno.

The remaining vehicles use their existing sprites until their exact original illustration has been matched and the existing wheel geometry verified. Do not substitute pictures of unrelated models.
