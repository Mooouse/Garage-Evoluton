# Idle Garage Evolution — design proposals

Designer: Claude (game-design agent, not the maker or the quality reviewer) · Date: 2026-10-20 · Played: gp dev, the 1.5.0 working tree (the developer was still editing it: Vite reloaded the page twice, at 4:18 and 8:23, and I didn't count those). I played 15 min as a new player at 1280×720 with the mouse, 16.7 min as a new player at 360×800 touch only, 4 min as an experienced player (`?garage=5&site=station`: shop, prestige, map, album, stats, the daily car) and one simulated next-day return (the save's `seen` set 20 h back). The new-player runs used a human-paced Playwright player. It scrubs where dirt is left, reads each card for 2.5 s, taps the gold Upgrade button whenever it shows and taps «Open it now!» when the goal is reached. Times are wall-clock from the page load.

What I measured in the two new-player sessions (desktop / phone): 22 / 30 cars. A RESTORED card after 24 of 26 / 32 of 34 cars: only 2 desktop cards had no «New model for the album!», so the 1.5.0 quiet path almost never fires in a first session. The wheel bay came on 21 of 22 desktop cars and took 14.4 s / 17.9 s on average, against 9.6 s / 5.1 s for the whole scrub, so the signature action is the shorter part of a car. The time from RESTORED to the next workable car was 6.6 s / 6.8 s per car. The next garage opened at 2:08 and 11:48 on desktop, and at 2:05, 7:52 and 13:24 on the phone.

## Top 3

1. [high] A RESTORED card only for real events: a new model becomes an album toast, and the next car is workable ≤ 3 s after RESTORED — playtime / session: on desktop 24 of 26 cards came only because every early car is «New model for the album!» (main.ts `results()` counts `p.newModel` as an event). Each card holds 2.6–5 s, then 6.6 s pass before the next car can be touched. That makes ~25 % of every 40 s car an exit point with nothing to do (desktop 0:13, 0:25, 0:53, 1:32 …). The demo bot's sheet shows the same stops at 0:11, 0:34, 0:56, 1:21 → in main.ts drop `p.newModel` from `event`. Fly the car's album thumbnail (`renderThumb`) into the Album button with «Album 14/142» (`Fx` numbers, `goalBanners` for the line). Keep the card for rare+ (`RARITIES.indexOf ≥ 2`), the ×2 offer, a rush job, the day's order and a goal just reached. Roll the next car in while the restored one drives out: overlap FLOW.finaleSec and FLOW.arriveSec in sim.ts so `surface` starts ≤ 3 s after `finale` · effort S
2. [high] The goal bar never goes backwards: a garage fund — playtime / session and D1 / coming back: the HUD goal is `min(money / price, cars / need)` (economy.ts `nextGoal`), and the gold Upgrade button spends the money. Corner Garage went 80 % → 3 % at 1:57. Tuning Workshop sat at 0–35 % from 2:10 to ~6:30 and fell to 0 % five times. Rally Works went 56 % → 0 % at 9:00, and on the phone Service Station went 50 % → 16 % at 15:58. The «next goal at 60–80 %» that money.returns promises is a dice roll at the moment the tab closes → in economy.ts give each garage-to-be a fund: 25 % of every payout goes into it automatically and can't be spent. Draw it in the HUD goal pill as a jar that fills with a coin trail (`Fx`). The pill shows `max(fund / price, cars / need)` with two ticks (cars 3/5 · fund 62 %). `bestBuy()` spends only the rest. The goal then only grows, and «Open it now!» comes when both are full. Keep `balance()` in bot.ts true to it and re-run `gp balance` · effort S
3. [high] Every robot earns while you're away, and the HUD says so from the first hire — D1 / coming back: `robotCarSecFor` returns null unless one garage's crew can finish a whole car alone (economy.ts: «a crew that can't finish a car alone earns nothing»). That needs the wash bot, grinder arm, polish drone and wrench bot in one garage ($1 720), plus the wheels, dents and parts. My next-day return after 4.5 min with a wash bot and a grinder arm got the streak screen and no «Welcome back» at all. On desktop the HUD line «Robots +$X/min» first showed at ~11:35, and on the phone not in 16 min. The genre's main return hook is missing for the players we need back → pay each robot for its trade's share of a car's value (a car-value fraction per cleared layer, the same table the sim uses). Show the welcome with what each robot did (`offlineEarnings` with its ×2 rewarded, `welcomeBack`). From the first hire, the HUD money pill says «Robots +$27/min · also while you're away» · effort S

## First minute

- [mid] The first purchase is taught on the gold Upgrade button in play, not in the shop panel — conversion / first minute: at 0:13 the first RESTORED card's coach hand points at Shop. A new player who follows it lands at 0:15 in a 5-card panel that reads «Power ×1.0 → ×1.1 · +<$1/min income», inside the minute CrazyGames counts. At 0:17 the play view shows the same purchase as a gold button anyway → in main.ts `showResults`, point `nudge()` / `coachHand` at the in-play quick-buy button (`data-gp=quick-buy`) after the card goes on. Show the Shop's coach only from the 4th car. The first sponge level visibly widens the brush ring under the cursor (scene.ts `drawPointer`), so the buy is seen on the car · effort S
- [mid] Bring the HUD buttons one at a time, each when it has something in it — conversion / first minute: at 0:37 the bottom bar grows from 1 button (Menu) to 6 at once (Shop with a red dot, Map, Album, Stats, Daily car, Menu) plus the gold Upgrade button. That's 7 new targets while the second car is being scrubbed → in main.ts `buildHud()`, show Shop after car 1, Album with the first album toast (Top 3 #1), Map with the first garage (~2:00), and Daily car when the first daily car is restored. Stats goes into the pause menu. Each arrival gets one `hints` line and a pop · effort S

## Session

- [high] Shorten and vary the wheel bay: one or two jobs per car, not all of them — playtime / session: the tyre bay ran on 21 of 22 desktop cars. Each wheel is 6–7 steps (nuts ×5, find the leak, plug, pump, balance, nuts ×5) and took 14.4 s on average on desktop and 17.9 s on the phone, longer than the whole scrub (9.6 s / 5.1 s). Car after car the core «layer under your finger» is the short part, and by 6–8 min it's «tap the nuts in order: 1, 2, 3…» again → in sim.ts / data.ts give each car's profile 1–2 of {wheels, dents, parts, engine}, never all four (a crash: dents + one wheel; a barn find: engine + parts; a flood: wheels). Each wheel job keeps 3 steps: nuts off, its one damage step, nuts on. With a Ratchet set, the nut steps become one tap per wheel (`nutsPerTap` already exists: raise it by wrench tier) · effort S
- [mid] A new garage keeps your tools: only the robots are the garage's own — playtime / session: opening Corner Garage at 2:08 (the first big reward) put the player back at a Level 0 sponge. The gold button offered «Upgrade Sponge $6», then the same $8 / $9 / $12 ladder again, and the wash bot had to be hired again ($120). It happened again at Tuning Workshop and at Rally Works (11:48: «Hire Wash bot $120», «Ratchet set $91»). The reward feels like a reset, and the hand gets weaker right after it (economy.ts `travel()` swaps `s.tools = st.tools`) → make `save.tools` global (the player's hand), keep `robots` per site (`Sites`). For a garage of a higher tier, its first new tool starts at the level the line had reached ÷ 2. Re-run `scripts/gp.sh balance` (the stages will be faster: raise the garage price or its car gate) · effort M
- [mid] A garage opening is a reveal, not four «???» boxes — playtime / session: the «New garage!» panel at 2:08 showed four «???» boxes and «NEW VEHICLES TO DISCOVER HERE: 9». In the first build I played it was also captioned «A cramped old shed: one bulb, one car» for the Corner Garage (it was gone by the phone run). Corner Garage then looks like the Shed with a green stripe → show the garage's 9 cars as silhouettes (`silhouetteThumb`, the `collection` look), with the first one rolling in through the door. Give each of the first garages one changed rule on the car that the player meets in the first car there, using the layers that exist (Corner: rust under every mud patch; Tuning: a respray colour pick on every car; Rally: gravel dents + mud) · effort M

## Coming back

- [high] The first session meets the rare car of the day, and tomorrow's is shown waiting — D1 / coming back: the rare car of the day only arrives when the player presses «Daily car» in the HUD (main.ts `showDaily`) or «Bring it in» on the title, which a first visit skips. In two 15-min new-player sessions it never came. The reason to return («a rare car of the day waits at the gate», money.returns) is never shown to the player who most needs it → in main.ts `makeCar()`, make the 6th car of a first session (≈ 3:00) today's daily car on its own, with the gate drama (horn, «The rare car of the day!»). Its card ends with tomorrow's: a covered silhouette, its rarity chip and «New rare car in 9 h» (`dailyCar(save, tomorrow)`). From then on the HUD «Daily car» button shows the countdown · effort S
- [mid] One return screen, one tap into the garage, and the goal left open on the title — D1 / coming back: on my next-day return the player tapped «Collect $974» (streak), then saw the title, then «Continue»: 2 taps. The title's daily card shows a generic star icon, not the car. Nothing says «Tuning Workshop 25 % · 3/5 cars», the goal I'd left → merge the streak, the robots' earnings and today's rare car into one card (`cards` / `mountStreakCard` / `welcomeBack`) with the car's picture (`renderThumb`) and one button «Collect & bring it in» that lands in play with the rare car rolling in. Add the goal pill to the title under Continue · effort S

## Phones

- [high] In phone landscape, size the car to the height left over, not to a fixed share of the width — conversion and playtime on mobile: at 800×360 (the QA's phone-landscape shot) the car is ~280 px of 800 px wide and ~100 px tall. The scrub, the dents and the wheel bay all happen in a strip a thumb covers. At 800×450 it is ~55 % of the width → in scene.ts `resize`, below 400 px of height put the HUD in one compact row (money · goal · condition) and the bottom bar down the right edge. Fit the car to the height left between the top row and the tool strip (≈ 1.6× today). The hint goes over the wall, not the roof · effort S
- [mid] In portrait, use the empty top half and take the hint off the car — playtime on mobile: at 360×800 the area from the car card (y 160) to the car's roof (y 430) is bare wall in every frame of my 16-min phone run. «Grab a dent and pull it out!» and «Grind off the rust!» sit on the roof the finger is scrubbing, and the bottom has 6 buttons plus the gold Upgrade button (~25 % of the screen). The owner rejected a pan, so → in scene.ts `resize` move the car up to the vertical centre, put the hint bubble in the wall space above it, and show the next car waiting behind the door (its silhouette) and the robots at a readable size there. Fold Map, Stats and Daily car into a «More» sheet (main.ts `buildHud`: Shop · Album · More · Menu, each ≥ 56 px) · effort S
- [mid] Load the shed's cars first, the other 112 pictures after Play — conversion on mobile: `gp qa` puts the slow phone at ≈ 14.7 s to a tappable Play (2 503 KB gzipped, 120 painted cars inline), against CrazyGames' 3 s. I agree with AUDIT.md that size isn't the only cause (1.0.0 at 143 KB converted 34.6 % on mobile), but mobile is where it costs most → a block request: a CrazyGames build with assets next to index.html, loaded after the first frame, or a streamed asset pack. Until then, in painted-art.ts decode only the current garage's pool (8 cars) at boot and the rest in idle time · effort L

## Juice and feel

- [mid] Count the payout up beat by beat on RESTORED — conversion and playtime: the card's «+$486» just appears. The bonuses («Rush job done! ×2», «Perfect clean! +30%», «Perfect wheels +10%») are chips beside it, so the moment the player earned isn't played. gp qa's feel check: round:end heard 1 of 2 times, median 1 channel → `ScoreSequencer`: the base, then +30 % Perfect, then ×2 Rush, each with a rising coin tick (`createSfx` coin, pitch +6 % a beat), the land sized by the total. A coin trail into the money pill (`Fx`); a tap fast-forwards · effort S
- [mid] Every purchase shows on the tool and the car; a new tool tier gets a reveal — playtime / session: the player tapped the gold Upgrade button 145 times in 15 min on desktop. Each tap only scales the button (main.ts quick-buy: `scale(1.12)`) and plays `sound.buy()`. The Garden hose (5:27), the Orbital polisher (5:51) and the Pressure washer (10:29) came with only `sound.unlock()`: nothing drawn → a coin flies from the button to that tool's slot in the tool strip, the slot shows a level pip and the brush ring grows. At a tier (every `TIER_EVERY`) show «New tool: Pressure washer»: the tool drawn big (tool-art.ts) over the car for 1 s, then hung on the pegboard, and its first strokes use a new particle (foam, sparks, spray) · effort M
- [low] Raise the mix: gp qa measured −28.6 LUFS (effects −30.4) — conversion: the satisfying layer sounds (scrub, grinder sparks, polish squeak) are the twist's feel, and at that level they're under a laptop's speakers. Hold the mixer at about −18 LUFS, with the scrub loop above the music → sound.ts through `createMixer` levels; check with `gp qa`'s sound section · effort S

## Content and variety

- [mid] Rush jobs and orders change how the car is played, not only the pay — playtime / session: a rush job came every ~5 cars (1:37, 4:11, 10:07, 13:23 on desktop) and plays exactly like any car with a clock. The day's order («Today's order 0/3 · Barn find») only counts cars → in sim.ts `newCar`, a rush car comes pre-scrubbed of its mud (start at the rust), with a wider brush for its clock, so it reads as a sprint. An order car shows its profile's look (barn find: dust + engine; flood: soot + wheels) on the car card before it rolls in · effort S
- [low] The album's counters fit a first session — D1 / coming back: the album opens on «Versions 1/994 · Rare finds 0/568». Those numbers no session moves visibly, and they bury «Models 22/142» → show per garage («Corner Garage 7/9 · 2 rare»), with the garage's nearest set as the goal (`collection`, `mountAlbum`). The big totals go on the stats page · effort S

## The genre's hits

- Tiny Fishing (CrazyGames, 9.2, 168K likes): one cast, then three upgrade buttons always on the main screen, and one of them is offline earnings, so the player buys the reason to come back with their eyes on it → we take the «while you're away» value as a visible number on every robot purchase and in the HUD from the first hire (Top 3 #3). The return shows what each robot did.
- Leek Factory Tycoon (9.4, 219K likes): «automation is the key, not clicking». Every machine bought is visibly added to the line, and the factory earns offline → we take that from the first robot: a robot earns at once and alone (Top 3 #3), and is drawn working at a readable size on the phone, not as an icon in a corner.
- Crash Test Idle (9.6, 11K likes): one action whose spectacle grows with every upgrade (a faster car, a bigger crash, more money flying) → we take the tool tiers as visible steps of the same scrub: a reveal and a new stroke particle per tier, the brush ring growing per level (Juice and feel #2).
- Idle Car Service: Tycoon (9.3, July 2026): each new area brings a new *service* (a new verb: refuel, drive-thru, car wash, cinema), not just more of the same → we take: each of the first garages changes one rule on the car (Session: «a reveal, not four ???»). Never a copy of their station loop.
- The MachinEGG (9.3, 25K likes): once the main goal is done, it offers speedrun, handicap challenges and an endless mode → a later update for us, after the prestige (Not now).

## Agreement with AUDIT.md (the developer's audit)

I agree with its diagnosis of rhythm (stops), the empty 100 % goal, the shop nobody finds (the gold Upgrade button works: my player bought 145 times in 15 min), rush jobs for tension, and the platform request for the upload's size. Where I go beyond or disagree:

- 1.5.0's card «only for events» still shows a card after almost every car in a first session, because «New model» is an event and every early car is new: 24 of 26 cards on desktop. The audit also counts a car at 15–20 s. I measured ~40 s, with 6.6 s of every car spent rolling out and in with nothing to touch (Top 3 #1).
- The goal still goes backwards. It's money-based, and the new gold Upgrade button is built to spend that money: 80 % → 3 %, 56 % → 0 % (Top 3 #2). `bestBuy`'s «keep half» guard only starts at 50 %.
- P3 #11 promises «robots will earn ~$Y tonight», but for most first-session players they earn $0: offline needs a full crew in one garage (Top 3 #3). The line also sits in the pause menu, which a CrazyGames player closing the tab never opens. The reason to return has to show in play (Coming back #1).
- Missed: opening a garage resets the hand's tools, and the wheel bay outweighs the scrub (Session #1, #2).

## Not now

- A leaderboard (Leek Factory's live one): no portal-wide one is a block yet, and a board of cars restored would reward idle time, not play.
- Modes after the prestige (MachinEGG's speedrun/endless): the first session doesn't reach the prestige (79.5 min by the balance bot).
- `seasonPass`, `luckyWheel`, `restockShop`, `dailyQuests`: each is a new screen. The game already has 14 screens, a streak, an order, a daily car and achievements, and what's missing is that the ones it has reach the new player (Coming back #1, #2).
- More garages or vehicles: 23 garages and 142 vehicles already outlast the session (the meta is 52 min in the model, 557 min for the bot). Deepen the first four instead.
- A panning camera on phones: the owner rejected it (AUDIT.md P2 #7). The phone proposals above work without one.
