# Idle Garage Evolution — quality review

Reviewer: second agent (Claude, did not make the game) · Date: 2026-10-10 (re-checks to 2026-10-20, last: 1.5.1 asset chapters, git 6c6d324, the production upload throttled; before that 1.5.0 design round, git ef5bfa4: 12.5 min new-player session at 1280×720, 75 s each at 390×844, 800×360 and 667×375, a next-day return) · Played: the upload build (upload/index.html, served locally) and `gp dev` (test hooks, `?seed=1..7`, `?lang=de/fr/ru/pl`, `?ads=fail`, `?garage=1..6`, `?model=`, `?profile=`, `?gallery=…`), about 25 min in all plus about 40 min on 1.3.0, with Playwright/Chromium. Most of it at 360×800 (touch context), plus 800×360, 800×450 and 1280×720. 16 cars by hand and with the demo bot: Shed → Workshop → close to the Service Station, the shop, robots, album, stats, pause, reload to the title, and the ×2 offer with ads working and with ads failing. For 1.3.0: every Earth garage through the Map, 8 of the 9 space garages (all but Jupiter), the album with every model found, the colour gallery at 2×, the tyre bay on painted cars, spacecraft landing, nozzle repair and take-off, achievements, and the sliders. I also looked at the fresh full-QA screenshots (263/263 after 27dd536), the covers and the 1920×1080 preview video. I could not hear the audio.

## Scores

Each screen of qa/review-sheet.png (1280×720, DPR 1) against the approved references.

- title: 4/5 — A finished game title in the game's own type (big cream title, cyan subline, drawn gold buttons, the rare car of the day, sliders, the language button), as finished as the approved Potion Logic or Starsilk titles. It isn't a 5 because the panel covers the dirty car and the room is dimmed almost to black, so the hero, which made the approved 1.0.0 shot, barely shows. Only returning players see it (a first visit lands in play).
- play-1: 4/5 — The painted Daily Glide fills the middle of a lit, layered shed (lamp pool, pegboard, shelves, roller door, floor reflection). The sparks show where the grinder works, the hint is a drawn pill, and the HUD is compact with readable 14 px text. As finished as the approved Candle Crypt play frame. The lower fifth of the floor is plain.
- play-2: 4/5 — The RESTORED finale: big gold outlined lettering and the pay over the car as it drives out into daylight. Lively and on-style.
- meta: 4/5 — The album has drawn cards with each car's picture, versions dots, silhouettes for unknown cars, drawn ◀ ▶ buttons and coloured counters. One style with play. The panel is plain dark glass, like the approved Hang the Moon result.
