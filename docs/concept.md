# Idle Garage Evolution — concept, originality check and the 1.0 spec

Source brief: game_platform `publisher/ideas/2026-10-10-garage-evolution.md` (GARAGE EVOLUTION, the owner's top proposal,
2026-10-10), with the platform's notes: vehicles are recognisable **archetypes**, never copies of real models, and never
misspelt brands; land in gameplay at once; the first restored car in 20–40 s; phone first.

## The owner's decisions (2026-10-10, relayed by the platform session)

1. **Real cars, only the names changed.** «расписывал же спецом точные копии машин как в реале сделать, только названия
   поменять — поменяй». This overrides the platform's note 1 of the brief: every vehicle is drawn as close as we can to
   one concrete real model (silhouette, proportions, lamps, grille, wheels, characteristic lines — the brief's section
   «МАКСИМАЛЬНАЯ ВИЗУАЛЬНАЯ ТОЧНОСТЬ»). To keep the portal risk down: no logos, badges or lettering of a make on any
   vehicle; the names are our own and never a misspelt brand («Porshe», «Ferari» are out; «Kestrel GT» is fine). The era
   and the country go into the album card's line ("German sports coupe of the 60s"), never into the name.
2. **Wheels are a job of their own** («Придумай механику с колёсами. Они тоже требуют каких-то действий от игрока»).
   A damaged wheel (flat, bald or bent) is part of a car's condition and is worked in the tyre bay with actions of its
   own, not plain clicks: undo the nuts in a star pattern (the order is lit; the right order keeps the Perfect bonus),
   the wheel comes off; a flat: spray water over the tyre until the leak bubbles, tap the bubbles to plug it; a bald tyre:
   pull a new one on; a bent rim: hammer the dents out; then pump by holding until the needle is in the green zone (too
   much — the tyre pops), balance it (tap when the heavy spot of the spinning wheel is on top, a weight goes on), fit it
   and tighten the nuts in the star order until they click. Every wheel gets a new rim: rims are collectible with a
   rarity (the album's rim row). The Wrench bot automates wheel jobs later; precise hand work gives "Perfect".
   In space: the same rhythm on landing gear (a later update; 1.0 keeps the wheel bay for rovers and the gear wheels).
3. **The owner's 50 cars** («хочу 50. Не 20», 2026-10-10 23:55): the 50 legendary cars from his list replace the game's
   passenger cars, ten a garage level by era and class, each changed a little and renamed (the table below; the whole
   list as a pool for updates: [cars-list.md](cars-list.md)). The wheel job and the rest of the design stay.

## The chosen idea

**One sentence:** bring wrecked cars back with your own hands — the car changes layer by layer under your finger (mud,
rust, primer, scratches, parts, engine), drives out RESTORED with its headlights on, and the garage grows from a shed toward
a space hangar.

**The twist:** the car is a stack of real layers, and a tap works on whatever surface is under the finger — the tool
switches by itself (brush → grinder → spray gun → polisher → wrench) and erases exactly where it touches. The first time a
layer is gone ("Rust under the mud") the player sees the next one appear and the tool change: `host.twist('layer-reveal')`.

| | Idle Garage Evolution | Retro Garage – Car Mechanic | Idle Car Service: Tycoon | Power-wash sims |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What the player does | scrubs, grinds, sprays, polishes and bolts directly on the car; the touched spot changes | picks parts in menus, swaps them | builds and upgrades stations, collects money | sprays dirt off surfaces |
| How progress shows | the car itself (layer by layer), robots appearing in the garage, the garage's rooms | a car's parts list | new stations on a map | a cleaner surface |
| The goal | the next garage level and the collection album (40 models, variants, rarities) | sell/keep cars | the next map | the next job |

The first 30 seconds and the preview video show the twist: mud scrubbed away, rust under it ground off with sparks, the shine
and the headlights, the drive-out.

## The 1.0 spec (what is in, what comes later)

**In 1.0 (an honest vertical slice of the brief):**
- **Earth, garage levels 1–5**: Shed → Workshop → Service Station → Luxury Garage → Industrial Plant. Each is a new room
  drawn in the scene (size, walls, lights, equipment), new vehicle classes, new work types and more robot slots.
- **The first jump to space** (level 6, Space Hangar) as the promise: visible and locked in the first life; the one
  prestige (International Chain) opens it. Four space vehicles in 1.0 (lunar rover, orbital shuttle, small rocket, cargo
  hauler) and the first "Unknown" object.
- **8 work types**: mud, dust (barn finds), soot (fire damage) — washed; rust — ground; paint (primer patches) — sprayed;
  scratches/oxidation — polished; parts (wheels, bumpers, mirror, headlight) — bolted with the wrench; engine — fixed in the
  diagnostic x-ray view. Then the finale: lights, horn, the engine, the gate, the drive-out, RESTORED +$.
- **40 models** as archetypes with our own names (no real model shapes, no near-brands), each with instances (#001 …) of
  their own condition, colour, variant (standard, racing, police, luxury, off-road, prototype, limited) and rarity (Common →
  Mythic; Unknown in space). Rare instances carry a story line ("Found after 30 years in a barn").
- **Collection album** (drawn cards with the car itself, ??? for undiscovered ones, variant and rarity progress) and
  **statistics** (drawn dashboard: totals, by class, records, area cleaned, rust removed, parts fitted, earned).
- **Automation**: robots bought per work type, visible beside the car, working on their own layer; they earn while away
  (offline earnings on return, ×2 for a rewarded ad).
- **Daily**: a rare car of the day waits at the gate (shown on the title), once per UTC day.
- **One prestige**: International Chain — sells the Earth garage for permanent stars (+income) and opens the space hangar.

**Later (updates):** the Moon (lunar dust), Mars (sand, radiation), interplanetary service with several garages at once,
further prestiges (Interplanetary Corporation, Galactic Service), eras beyond (interstellar, post-human), custom paint,
achievements, more models and variants.

## The progression

An idle game: a **level shop with tiers** (docs/guides/upgrades.md: idle → level shop, dozens of levels, milestones, later
prestige; second track: a collection, offline earnings).

| | Idle Garage Evolution | Retro Garage | Idle Car Service |
|---|---|---|---|
| What the currency buys | tool levels (each 10 levels a new tool tier with its own effect: sponge → hose → pressure washer …), robots (each one appears in the garage), garage levels | parts, cars | stations, upgrades |
| What a new purchase changes in play | a bigger, stronger stroke; a new tool and effect; a robot working beside you; a new room, new work types and vehicle classes | a car's stats | income numbers |
| The second track | the collection album (models × variants × rarities), statistics, the daily rare car, prestige stars | — | maps |
